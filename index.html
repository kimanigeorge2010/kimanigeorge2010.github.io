About Kimani George

Great creative work is never an accident. It is the product of clear strategy, deep craft, and the courage to say something that matters.

I am a Creative Producer and Brand Strategist with over 30 years of experience — beginning with published children's book illustration in 1994 and spanning senior-level campaign leadership across East Africa and, increasingly, for international brands.

My work sits at the intersection of strategic thinking and visual craft. I've led national television campaigns reaching millions of viewers, developed integrated brand strategies for some of the region's most recognised names, and competed on a global stage — earning five top-winner placements on eYeka, including two 1st place jury prizes for Roundup® (Bayer) and IKE's Herbicide (USA), judged directly by brand clients across a field from 46+ countries.

In recent years, I've expanded my practice into AI-driven visual development — combining emerging creative technologies with the rigorous strategic foundation I've built over three decades. The result is work that moves faster, reaches further, and retains the craft that separates memorable campaigns from forgettable ones.
